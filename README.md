## 🧑‍💻 About me

- 🧠 **Tech Lead DevOps & AI at [Orange](https://www.orange.com) DSI.** I built and run KVN, Orange's internal AI assistant: RAG and GraphRAG over 115 internal sources (Docling, BGE-M3, Qdrant, LangChain, open-source LLMs), guarded by Langfuse and LLM Guard, on a bi-site Docker Swarm. 200+ active users, 3,000+ conversations.
- 🧳 **Co-founder & CTO of [BoxSpot](https://boxspot.ma)**, with Badr Ragbi. Luggage storage at verified partner shops across Morocco: 18 points in 10 cities, 132 merchants, 5.0 on Google, picked by Morocco 300 for GITEX Africa. I own the platform end to end, from the FastAPI and PostGIS backend to the partner app on iOS and Android.
- 🌟 **Co-founder & CTO of [Northstars](https://northstars.studio).** We build products, then we run them. Engineering, infrastructure and on-call, from Tanger.
- ⚙️ **Building DevCloud**, a self-hosted Vercel-style platform on Kubernetes (Go operator, Knative, Tekton, kpack, ArgoCD) that hosts everything above.
- 🛰️ On the side: a four-site high-availability Kubernetes platform with an AI-assisted operator, YOLOv11 computer vision running 24/7, and an OCPP 1.6 backend for an EV-charging network.
- 🎓 **ESILV engineer, Data & AI**, Paris.
- 💬 Ask me about RAG in production, Kubernetes operators, FastAPI, Next.js, or running a platform as a one-person SRE team.
