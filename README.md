## 🧑‍💻 About me

- ☁️ **Cloud Engineer at [Orange](https://www.orange.com)**. I take AI use cases from notebook to production on GCP and Kubernetes.
- 🧳 **Founder of [BoxSpot](https://boxspot.ma)**. Luggage storage at verified partner shops across Morocco, from 9 MAD an hour.
- 🌟 **Founder of [Northstars](https://northstars.studio)**. An embedded product studio in Tanger: we become your product department.
- ⚙️ **Building DevCloud**. A self-hosted, Vercel-style platform on Kubernetes that runs everything above.
- 🎓 **Software engineering student at [ESILV](https://www.esilv.fr)**, Paris.
- 💬 Ask me about Kubernetes operators, Knative, FastAPI, Next.js, or shipping LLM features that survive production.
